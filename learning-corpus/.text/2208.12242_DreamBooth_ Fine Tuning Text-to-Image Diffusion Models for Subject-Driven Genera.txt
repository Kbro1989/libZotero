   We apply our approach to a myriad of text-based im-
age generation applications including recontextualization of
subjects, modification of their properties, original art rendi-
tions, and more, paving the way to a new stream of pre-
viously unassailable tasks. We highlight the contribution
of each component in our method via ablation studies, and
compare with alternative baselines and related work. We
also conduct a user study to evaluate subject and prompt
fidelity in our synthesized images, compared to alternative
approaches.

4.4. Applications

Recontextualization We can generate novel images for a
specific subject in different contexts (Figure 7) with descrip-
tive prompts ("a [V] [class noun] [context description]").
Importantly, we are able to generate the subject in new
poses and articulations, with previously unseen scene struc-
ture and realistic integration of the subject in the scene (e.g.
contact, shadows, reflections).

Art Renditions Given a prompt "a painting of a [V] [class
noun] in the style of [famous painter]" or "a statue of a [V]
[class noun] in the style of [famous sculptor]" we are able
to generate artistic renditions of our subject. Unlike style
transfer, where the source structure is preserved and only
the style is transferred, we are able to generate meaning-
