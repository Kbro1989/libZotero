                                                                                          1. Introduction

                                              Throughout this paper, R always denotes a non-trivial associative ring with identity.
                                          All modules are unital left R-modules. We denote the category of all left R-modules by
                                          R-Mod. The full subcategory of R-Mod consisting of all Gorenstein projective R-modules
                                          (resp. all projective R-modules) are denoted by GP(R) (resp. P(R)). We use pd(M )
                                          and Gpd(M ) to denote, respectively, the projective dimension and Gorenstein projective
                                          dimension of M . We always assume that X is a class of R-modules which contains all
                                          projective R-modules.

                                              As a generalization of finitely generated projective modules over a noetherian ring R,
                                          Auslander and Bridger [3] introduced in 1969 the notion of the module with G-dimension
                                          zero. The G-dimension for finitely generated R-module M , denoted by G-dim(M ), is
                                          defined via resolution with this kind of module. They proved that G-dim(M )  pd(M ),
                                          with equality G-dim(M ) = pd(M ) when pd(M ) is finite. Furthermore, they give a char-
                                          acterization of the Gorenstein local rings in terms of G-dimension in [2]. Namely, R is
                                          Gorenstein if its residue field k has finite G-dimension, and only if every finitely generated
                                          R-module has finite G-dimension.
