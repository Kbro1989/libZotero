F.3 Computing Resources

four NVIDIA A100 80GB GPUs, 32GB system
memory, and a 128-core AMD CPU. All training
runs use CUDA 12.8, PyTorch 2.7.1, and Deep-
Speed 0.18.2.

F.4 Training Cost Analysis

GRPO and SAVE under the same experimental
setting, both using DeepSpeed ZeRO-2 with CPU

memory during the backward pass and 26 hours
of training, while SAVE requires 62GB of peak
GPU memory and 29 hours. The additional mem-
ory mainly comes from maintaining and updating
the reward model, including the value head. By
