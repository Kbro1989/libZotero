                                        the user's persona. Such responses may be inappropriate or                     Robot: I am from Guangzhou.
                                        even offensive, which may lead to the bad user experience.

                                        user and robot personas into dialogue generation via designing                 Robot: Yes, I want to go to Guangzhou.
                                        a dynamic persona-aware fusion method. To bridge the gap
                                        between the learning objective function and evaluation metrics,                Our model with bilateral personas

                                        rion is adopted with contrastive learning to select the proper                 User: Where are you from?
                                        response from the generated candidates. Moreover, a bilateral
                                        persona accuracy metric is designed to measure the degree of                   Robot: I come from Guangzhou.
                                        bilateral personalization. Experimental results demonstrate that,
                                        compared with several state-of-the-art methods, the final results              User: Come to my city and play with me?
                                        of the proposed method are more personalized and consistent
