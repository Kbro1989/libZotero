                          2. METHODOLOGY

This section describes the Improved Saliency Criterion (ISC) and
the sensitivity-aware mixed sparsity pruning method. ISC serves the
purpose of selecting the optimal weight elements to be pruned, and
the sensitivity level facilitates the allocation of the pruning ratios for
different layers.
