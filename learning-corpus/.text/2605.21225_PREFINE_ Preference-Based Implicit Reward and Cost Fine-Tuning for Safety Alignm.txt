                                                                            Discussion These results illustrate a fundamental shift: rather than labori-
                                                                            ously engineering cost functions or performing expensive online interac-
                                                                            tions, we can leverage naturally occurring preferences to retrofit safety into
                                                                            a differentiable pre-trained policy quickly and robustly. PREFINE thus paves
                                                                            a practical path towards safe deployment in domains where data is not easily
                                                                            available and safety is non-negotiable such as autonomous driving systems
                                                                            (reducing collisions) to healthcare (avoiding dangerous drug interactions).
                                                                            However, policy-sampled counterfactual actions for computing DPO loss
                                                                            can introduce noise when sampled actions are not strictly safer/more unsafe.
                                                                            SFT regularization mitigates but does not eliminate this. Future work should
                                                                            explore ways to mitigate adversarial or erroneous feedback.
